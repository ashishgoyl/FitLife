# FitLife Features Overview

FitLife is an all-in-one personal health, nutrition, and wellness tracking platform crafted for modern lifestyle routines with special focus on Indian foods, macro precision, and daily habits.

---

## 🌟 Key Functional Modules

### 1. 🔥 Calorie Budget & Macro Pacing
- **Circular SVG Budget Ring**: Visual progress indicator depicting calories consumed vs target budget.
- **Dynamic Deficit / Surplus**: Calculates calories remaining or pacing surplus based on selected user goals (*Weight Loss*, *Muscle Gain*, *Maintenance*).
- **Macro Breakdown**: Real-time progress bars for Protein, Carbohydrates, and Fats with gram-level targets.

### 2. 🍛 Comprehensive Indian Foods Library
- Curated database of over 200+ popular Indian dishes (Rotis, Paneer curries, Dals, Biryani, Idli/Dosa, Street foods, Sweets, and Beverages).
- **Unit & Portion Scaling**: Switch between standard servings, grams, cups, pieces, or katoris with automated nutrition recalculation.
- **Custom Food Addition**: Add custom recipes and ingredients directly to your local library.

### 3. 📸 QR & Barcode Scanner
- Built-in camera scanner with live canvas viewfinder.
- Connects directly with the **Open Food Facts API** to instantly fetch verified product nutrition by scanning packaged food barcodes.

### 4. 💧 Smart Hydration Tracker
- Visual 250ml glass counters with animated fill effects.
- Configurable daily intake target (default: 2,000ml to 3,500ml).
- Daily streak tracking and milestone toasts.

### 5. 🌙 Sleep Recovery Tracker
- Bedtime and wake-time logging with restorative recovery calculation.
- 7-day SVG bar history graph showcasing weekly sleep trends.
- Integrated simulated wearable sync for smartwatches.

### 6. 🏋️ Exercise & Workout Routine
- MET-based calorie burn calculator across cardiovascular, strength, and yoga routines.
- Pre-loaded database of exercises with detailed instructions and targeted muscle groups.
- Set, repetition, and duration tracking.

### 7. ⚖️ Weight Progression
- Log daily body weight with trend line graphs.
- BMI calculation and goal progress pacing.

### 8. 🤖 AI Health Coach (Gemini Flash)
- Synthesizes all daily logged metrics (calories, protein, water, workouts, sleep) into actionable, uplifting daily advice.
- Powered by Google Gemini 3.1 Flash with multi-model fallback.
- Operates 100% offline via built-in health advice heuristics when an API key is not supplied.

### 9. 🛠️ Admin Management Console
- Accessible at `/admin.html`.
- Browse all registered users and session stats.
- Manage Indian food database items and inspect system telemetry.
